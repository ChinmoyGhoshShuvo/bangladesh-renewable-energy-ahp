**Title:** Ranking Renewable Energy Sources for Bangladesh (AHP)

**Summary:** For my bachelor's thesis at KUET, I combined a survey of the public and sector experts with an Analytic Hierarchy Process that scored solar, biomass, wind and tidal energy on maturity, reliability, cost and sustainability. Solar ranked first (priority 0.481). The survey (188 public and 21 expert responses) found that financial constraints were the most-cited barrier to adoption.
